# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space.

## The short version

- Be kind and patient. Many people who use this tool are not programmers, and no question about it is too basic.
- Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person.
- No harassment, no insults, no sexual language or imagery, no publishing of anyone's private information.
- Never post someone else's notes, or your own if they mention other people.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here breaks these rules, tell the maintainers privately. Open a private report at <https://github.com/HeroForgeAI/vault-mirror/security/advisories/new> and begin the title with "Conduct". Only the maintainers can read it.

Every report is read and handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may block a person who keeps breaking them. The enforcement steps in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) are the guide.
